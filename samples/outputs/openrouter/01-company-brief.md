> What is happening with Northwind Robotics?

## Executive summary

Northwind Robotics raised $120 million in Series D funding to expand manufacturing and engineering. It subsequently recalled about 2,000 warehouse robots over a battery overheating defect, with an estimated cost of $8 million. The company also signed a multi-year partnership to automate 40 Brightmart distribution centers. A low-credibility outlet reported unconfirmed acquisition talks with Titan Industrial.
