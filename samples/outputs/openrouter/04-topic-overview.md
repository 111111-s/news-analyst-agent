> Overview of the Port of Marisol situation and its effect on shipping

## Executive summary

Dockworkers at the Port of Marisol struck after talks over wages and automation broke down. About 60 container ships waited offshore in the first week, while diversions added several days to delivery times. Retailers warned of holiday delays as freight rates rose and some moved shipments to more expensive air freight. The strike ended with a tentative deal, and port operators subsequently expected the backlog to clear by early December.
